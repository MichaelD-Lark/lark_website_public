Sparkling Rosé

A field blend of Ortega, Bacchus, Pinot noir, Pinot gris, Chardonnay, Auxerrois, Pinot blanc, and Pineau d'aunis

The positive result of a very difficult vintage, in which tiny volumes required us to blend everything together to make a single cuvée.
Co-fermented and left on the fine lees for eleven months. Re-fermented in bottle, without disgorgement. 

A light blush with added
complexity and finesse due to the diverse blend of varieties.

Oh, and say "Hello" to our resident Polled Dorsets, aka our landscaping and fertilising team!

ABV: 10.5%
